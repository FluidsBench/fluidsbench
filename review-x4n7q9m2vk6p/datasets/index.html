Automotive external aerodynamics

AhmedML

High-fidelity, time-averaged data from transient hybrid RANS–LES simulations of 500 parametrically varied Ahmed bodies, with native three-dimensional boundary and volume fields, geometry, force coefficients, slices, images, and deterministic data-efficiency and out-of-distribution splits.

Automotive external aerodynamics

DrivAerML

High-fidelity, time-averaged data from scale-resolving simulations of 500 designed DrivAer notchback variants. The public release currently provides 484 complete CFD cases, native surface and volume fields, geometry, forces, diagnostic images, and eight official split families.

Automotive external aerodynamics

DrivAerNet++

A large multimodal automotive-aerodynamics collection with 8,150 vehicle designs spanning fastback, notchback, and estateback categories, including surface and volume CFD, meshes, coefficients, annotations, point clouds, and rendered or sketched geometry representations.

Automotive external aerodynamics

WindsorML

High-fidelity wall-modelled LES data for 355 designed Windsor-body variants, with approximately 300 million cells per simulation and time-averaged boundary, volume, geometry, force, slice, and image products.

Submission status ↗ Dataset guide →

High-lift aircraft aerodynamics

HiLiftAeroML

A 1,800-case wall-modelled LES dataset formed by 180 NASA CRM-HL geometry variants at ten angles of attack from 4° to 22°, with 300–500 million-cell solution-adapted meshes and native boundary, volume, geometry, force, and visual products.

Submission status ↗ Dataset guide →

Two-dimensional airfoil aerodynamics

AirfRANS

A 1,000-case steady incompressible RANS dataset over NACA 4- and 5-digit airfoils, spanning Reynolds numbers from 2 to 6 million and angles of attack from −5° to 15°, with official standard, scarce-data, and extrapolation tasks.

Submission status ↗ Dataset guide →

Blended-wing-body aerodynamics

BlendedNet

A public surface-aerodynamics dataset with 999 unique blended-wing-body geometries and 8,830 converged steady RANS cases across geometry and flight-condition variation, using 9–14 million volume cells per simulation.

Turbine-cascade aerodynamics

VKI-LS59

A PLAID-formatted collection of 839 two-dimensional compressible RANS simulations of the LS59 turbine cascade, spanning inlet incidence and outlet Mach number with geometry, signed-distance, field, curve, and scalar quantities.

Compressor aerodynamics

Rotor37

A PLAID-formatted collection of 1,200 three-dimensional RANS simulations of the NASA Rotor 37 compressor blade, with geometry and operating-condition inputs and distributed and integrated compressor outputs.
