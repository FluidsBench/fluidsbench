High-lift aircraft aerodynamics

Overview

A 1,800-case wall-modelled LES dataset formed by 180 NASA CRM-HL geometry variants at ten angles of attack from 4° to 22°, with 300–500 million-cell solution-adapted meshes and native boundary, volume, geometry, force, and visual products.

FluidsBench evaluation schematic

  1. Model inputs CRM-HL geometry + angle of attack
  2. Scoring support Native aircraft boundary and retained valid volume points
  3. Reported evaluation Native field errors, integrated loads, ten Cp rows and five velocity stations

Schematic only; the benchmark specification fixes the exact files, entities, arrays, and equations.

Technical details

Scoring source pin: nvidia/HiLiftAeroML @ bbec30b; closed-candidate evaluator @ 68899f7. Open contract.

What the source contains

Compressed native boundary and volume VTUs, STL and STEP geometry, force/reference tables, surface renders and convergence plots, fourteen deterministic split families, data-quality masks, a native-point boundary dual-area sidecar for every case, and a provenance manifest for six corrected mean force/moment records.

What FluidsBench currently evaluates

The closed schema-v3 candidate binds all fourteen official splits, complete native surface and retained valid volume fields, integrated loads, ten Cp rows and five velocity stations. Compact-v2 is the sole selected participant profile format. Twenty-three real-model previews and plot-only CFD truth for all 1,355 evaluation cases are available. Public intake remains closed pending final source-content pins, scoring-support publication and owner approval.

Current benchmark status

HiLiftAeroML is a closed schema-v3 candidate. All fourteen official evaluation labels are bound to eight exact ordered case sets, covering 1,355 unique cases. The evaluator is frozen at revision 68899f7 for local candidate dry runs. Public submissions remain closed pending the final source-content pins, scoring-support publication and benchmark-owner approval.

The development leaderboard contains 23 retained real-model previews: eleven Transolver and twelve GeoTransolver results. These are pre-release references, not official or citable benchmark results. Public Cp and velocity plot truth covers all 1,355 cases; that plotting derivative omits scoring weights and cannot replace the evaluator's separate scoring-truth release.

Dataset summary

Geometry
NASA CRM-HL high-lift aircraft with parameterized slat and flap deflections and gaps.
Cases
1,800 simulations: 180 geometry variants across 10 angles of attack from 4 degrees to 22 degrees.
Solver
Fidelity Charles explicit unstructured finite-volume solver with Fidelity Stitch Voronoi meshing.
Fidelity
Wall-Modeled Large-Eddy Simulation (WMLES) with solution-adapted grids.
Flow conditions
Mach 0.2 and chord-based Reynolds number 1.6 x 106.
Mesh scale
Solution-adapted grids between roughly 300M and 500M control volumes; exported volume meshes are octree-based.
License
CC BY 4.0, as stated by the dataset source.

Getting started

Start with the source documentation and split metadata, then download one representative case before planning a full transfer. The commands below pin the source snapshot audited for this page. For leaderboard work, always use the separate scoring source pin and case lists in the FluidsBench benchmark specification.

1. Download lightweight metadata first

python3 -m pip install -U huggingface_hub hf_xet
hf download nvidia/HiLiftAeroML \
  --repo-type dataset \
  --revision bbec30bcfc6103309c1375c5228b3ad0a586bfaf \
  --include "README.md" \
  --include "splits/**" \
  --include "data_quality/**" \
  --include "force_mom_all.csv" \
  --include "force_mom_surface_overrides_v1.json" \
  --include "geo_values_all.csv" \
  --local-dir ./hiliftaeroml

2. Preview one large native case

hf download nvidia/HiLiftAeroML \
  --repo-type dataset \
  --revision bbec30bcfc6103309c1375c5228b3ad0a586bfaf \
  --include "geo_LHC001_AoA_4/boundary_geo_LHC001_AoA_4.vtu.tgz" \
  --include "geo_LHC001_AoA_4/boundary_dual_area_geo_LHC001_AoA_4.npy" \
  --include "geo_LHC001_AoA_4/force_mom_geo_LHC001_AoA_4.csv" \

The full repository is about 66.8 TB as stored and expands further after extraction. The example boundary archive alone is approximately 12 GB and its area sidecar approximately 546 MB. Remove --dry-run only after checking local capacity. Each sidecar is little-endian float32 in square inches and matches the exact native boundary-point order; a constant area-unit conversion cancels from normalized weighted metrics.

Conditions and sampling

Operating conditions

Each geometry is simulated at ten angles of attack from 4° to 22° in 2° steps, at Mach 0.2 and chord Reynolds number 1.6 × 10⁶.

Sampling

Latin hypercube sampling supplies 180 geometry variants, crossed with the ten-angle sweep for 1,800 CFD cases.

Benchmark coverage

What the current splits test

Fourteen official split labels distinguish data efficiency, unseen geometry, single-angle tasks, angle extrapolation, high-deflection geometry, and stall behaviour. FluidsBench binds eight exact ordered evaluation case sets covering 1,355 unique cases; public submissions remain closed.

Source correction: mean force and moment coefficients

The 2 September 2026 source release corrects the mean cd, cl, cm, clp, clv, cdp, and cdv values for six cases. The replacements are canonically integrated from the released time-averaged surface pressure and wall-shear fields; the meshes, fields, geometry, split manifests, and the other 1,794 cases are unchanged.

The affected cases are geo_LHC012_AoA_16, geo_LHC018_AoA_16, geo_LHC028_AoA_18, geo_LHC028_AoA_22, geo_LHC129_AoA_4, and geo_LHC172_AoA_22. Their retained *_stdev, *_stderr, and *_ci95 columns are original solver-monitor diagnostics, not uncertainty estimates for the replaced means. See the source correction manifest for the exact old and new values, method, and hashes.

These tables document source provenance. The candidate evaluator obtains both prediction and truth loads by integrating the corresponding complete native surface fields with the same convention. It does not substitute the corrected CSV values for the scored truth loads.

Prepare a HiLiftAeroML candidate result Native inference, packaging and local validation

Public submissions are closed. The current workflow supports local or benchmark-owner-coordinated schema-v3 dry runs. Follow the HiLiftAeroML participant guide and its package configuration and commands.

  1. Select one official split label and use its exact ordered evaluation cases. Train and select checkpoints using the matching source training and validation definitions; evaluation fields must not influence fitting or preprocessing statistics.
  2. Run your model to predict pressure and three-component wall shear at every native boundary point, and pressure and three-component velocity at every retained valid volume point. All four fields are required. Map any different model representation onto these supports and document that mapping; the evaluator derives loads and profiles from the same complete predictions.
  3. Run the frozen evaluator over every case, accumulating chunk statistics before complete-case and split reductions. Retain its case receipts, metrics and profile products. The assembler requires the separately provided candidate scoring truth/support; the public browser plot bundle cannot supply this dependency.
  4. Assemble the package with the documented HiLiftAeroML adapter. Include the method and training regime, raw loaded checkpoint hashes, measured training/inference compute, discretization records, case metrics, prediction profiles and evaluation evidence. Full native prediction files and public code or model artifacts are optional.

Compact-v2 is the sole selected participant profile format. Each case submits a prediction-only NPZ with compact Cp values and velocity predictions; geometry, masks, weights and truth remain in evaluator-owned support. The existing contract identifiers retain their historical candidate suffix. Selecting this format does not open public intake or complete the remaining evaluator-release approval.

For an assembled compact-v2 candidate, validate locally from the submission repository:

python scripts/validate_submission.py \
  --candidate-dry-run \
  --profile-support-release /path/to/hiliftaeroml-compact-profile-support-v2-candidate \
  /path/to/hiliftaeroml-my-method-v2

A passing dry run validates the local package. It does not approve, register or publish a result. Use the candidate route while intake is closed; --contributor-stage becomes appropriate only after an official scoring-support release opens. Remaining source, support publication and owner-review requirements are recorded in the activation checklist.

Submitters run inference and the evaluator. Required maintainer validation checks the submitted package, exact coverage and hash chain; it does not rerun the model or recompute base metrics from full prediction fields. Approval of an official result remains a separate step.

Scoring support and complete coverage Exact files, weighting and complete-case rules

Submissions are currently closed.

Final predictions must cover the complete reference support. For every scored case, this means every required entity, exactly once, in the actual field-bearing mesh or curve file from the named public dataset release. FluidsBench binds those files, their ordering, any masks, and their hashes to the scoring-support release. A method may infer on another representation and may process a large file in chunks, but it must map its predictions back to every required reference entity before calculating the final metrics. Downsampled or partially covered predictions are not complete results.

How to calculate large cases

  • Use the published native nodal dual area for primary surface relative L2, and retain equal-node surface relative L2 as a secondary diagnostic. Each retained valid volume point contributes one unit weight; this candidate has no dual-volume weighting requirement.
  • Score relative errors on (P-p_inf)/q_inf, tau_wall/q_inf and U/|U_inf|. For vector relative L2, one point weight multiplies the squared three-component magnitude. Apply the raw volume validity mask before normalization and preserve the retained raw point IDs through chunking.
  • In one pass over each case, accumulate the squared-error numerator and ground-truth denominator for every relative-L2 variant required in the table above, together with the corresponding entity count and total physical measure wherever one is used. Chunks may be any convenient size, but their numerators and denominators must be summed before taking the square root; never average chunk-level L2 values.
  • Calculate each complete-case percentage first, then macro-average the case percentages so every case counts equally.

Sharing complete prediction fields is optional. A contributor may link versioned predictions in an external public repository, but the submission package records exact coverage plus the per-case sufficient statistics used to reproduce each required relative-L2 value.

Metric definitions

Relative L2 error
For each evaluation case, use the nondimensional field bases below and calculate \(100\sqrt{\sum_i w_i\lVert\hat q_i-q_i\rVert^2 / \sum_i w_i\lVert q_i\rVert^2}\). Surface primary metrics use the published nodal dual areas, with equal-node values as secondary diagnostics. Volume metrics use one unit per retained valid native point; no volume-weighted secondary metric is required. Sum the numerator and denominator across all chunks before taking the square root, then macro-average the complete-case percentages equally.
Relative L1 error
Supplementary relative L1 uses the same nondimensional fields and primary spatial weights. For vectors, accumulate absolute errors and absolute truth over all three components. Calculate the complete-case percentage before the equal-case macro average.
Field bases and dimensional diagnostics
Relative L1/L2 use (P-p_inf)/q_inf, tau_wall/q_inf and U/|U_inf|, using each case's freestream references. Undo model-specific standardization to reach these bases. Separate MAE/RMSE diagnostics are inverse-scaled per case to Pa for pressure and wall shear, and m/s for velocity, before equal-case aggregation. Vector relative L2 uses the full three-component magnitude; vector MAE/RMSE use the evaluator's per-component convention.
Surface pressure relative L1/L2
Relative L1/L2 for Cp = (P-p_inf)/q_inf at every native boundary point. This is a full-surface field metric; the separate Cp-cut metric uses evaluator-derived one-dimensional traces.
Surface wall-shear relative L1/L2
Relative L1/L2 for all three components of tau_wall/q_inf at every native boundary point, with the published nodal dual areas.
Volume velocity relative L1/L2
Relative L1/L2 for the three-component vector U/|U_inf| on every retained native volume point. The evaluator retains a point when raw Float32 avg(P) != 0.0, before normalization; predictions cannot change this mask.
Volume pressure relative L1/L2
Relative L1/L2 for (P-p_inf)/q_inf on the same retained valid volume points, each with one equal weight.
Coefficient of determination
\(R^2 = 1 - \mathrm{SSE}/\mathrm{SST}\), using the weights, truth-centering groups and case aggregation specified for each metric below. Higher is better; 1.0 is perfect. Profile metrics do not use one mean across all cases and samples.
Cd and Cl R2
Separate equal-case R2 values over the selected split. The evaluator integrates pressure and viscous loads from the complete native surface prediction and uses the same integration for truth. It applies each case's qRef, areaRef and angle convention, including the q_inf/qRef conversion exactly once; a separately predicted force head cannot replace this integration.
Load diagnostics
Drag, lift and pitching-moment MAE are required diagnostics. Pitching moment uses exact degree-two surface integration about forcesCoR with qRef * areaRef * chordRef normalization. It has no additional composite weight.
Cp cut R2
Within each case, center truth independently in every connected physical cut graph. Use physical arc-length weights and pool graph SSE and SST across rows A–J to calculate one case R2. Macro-average those complete-case R2 values equally across the split. The leaderboard station selector chooses a displayed row; it does not restrict scoring to that row.
Velocity profile R2
Compare |U| / |U_inf| at B.2, B.3, C.1, C.2 and C.3. Center truth within each station, use physical polyline arc-length weights and pool station SSE and SST within the case. Macro-average the complete-case R2 values equally. Invalid rows and gaps follow the frozen support and receive no invented values or connecting edges.

Candidate overall score

The closed candidate computes a bounded 0–100 score with 50% field, 25% force and 25% profile weight. These preview values are not official leaderboard claims. Each field error E_q below is a relative-L2 percentage on the stated nondimensional basis.

Relative L1, dimensional MAE/RMSE and pitching-moment MAE are supplementary diagnostics. Complete regional reports may also be included at zero weight; none of these adds a component to the score below. 

S_error(q) = 100 * max(0, 1 - E_q / cap_q)
S_R2(q)    = 100 * min(1, max(0, R2_q))
S_overall  = sum(weight_q * S_q)
